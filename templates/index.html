How the project was built

  1. 01Three datasets

    569 FNA cases (Wisconsin), 277k histology patches (IDC), 3,103 CBIS-DDSM mammograms.

  2. 02Digital Twin

    8 algorithms compared by 10-fold CV; a standardised RBF-SVM won (97.7% test accuracy).

  3. 03Histology CNN

    EfficientNet-B0 fine-tuned on patches, split by patient so test patients are unseen.

  4. 04Mammogram CNN

    Breast-cropped, CLAHE-enhanced full mammograms; official CBIS-DDSM test split.

  5. 05Explainability

    Class-activation heatmaps and feature-level drivers show why each result was given.

  6. 06Clinical pathway

    Scores become BI-RADS / pathology / cytology categories with referral advice and a printable report.

    Understanding breast cancer

    Know the disease. Catch it early.

    Breast cancer occurs when breast cells grow uncontrollably. It is one of the most common cancers in women worldwide, and men can develop it too.

    01

    Causes & risk factors

    • Genetic factors (e.g. BRCA1/BRCA2)
    • Hormonal factors
    • Age and family history
    • Lifestyle: alcohol, inactivity, weight
    02

    Effects

    • Physical and emotional impact
    • Changes in body image
    • Risk of metastasis to other organs
    03

    Prevention & screening

    • Regular screening and self-checks
    • Healthy lifestyle choices
    • Genetic testing if at high risk
    04

    Benign vs malignant

    Benign

    Non-cancerous. Cells look closer to normal, edges are smooth and regular, and the tumour does not spread.

    Malignant

    Cancerous. Nuclei are larger and irregular, with concave, jagged boundaries. These cells invade nearby tissue and can metastasise.

    05

    BI-RADS: how findings are graded and acted on

    The ACR Breast Imaging Reporting and Data System puts every mammogram, ultrasound or MRI finding into one category, each with a standard next step. In this app the category is suggested by the model; a radiologist assigns the final one.
